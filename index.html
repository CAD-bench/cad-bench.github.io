Overview

On the right-angle gearbox task, 31 of 32 agent submissions passed the build check, yet their mean task score was 4.0%. CAD-bench tests generated geometry against mechanical requirements, including hole alignment, thread fit and motion through a gear train.

The 17 tasks range from dimensioned solids and feature-rich parts to threaded pairs and gear assemblies. The grader checks dimensions, placement and mating geometry. Blender rigid-body simulations test gearbox contact, rotation direction and output speed.

Results

Across the 32 complete agent runs, the mean score on the four functional tasks was 14.1%. The 19 standalone-model runs averaged 3.7% on those tasks. All entries on the leaderboard were evaluated for the full set of 17 tasks.

Scoring

The overall score is the weighted average of the average scores for the Easy, Medium, Hard and Extreme levels, with respective weights 1, 2, 3 and 4. The Extreme level consists of the four functional tasks. Scores measure average credit, not the percentage of fully correct designs.

Scorer validation

The scorer audit used independently constructed valid models and deliberately altered designs. It found a valid rotated hex nut rejected by a reference-shape filter. Some small dimensional errors retained high scores. The paper and validation records document these cases to distinguish model errors from grader errors.
